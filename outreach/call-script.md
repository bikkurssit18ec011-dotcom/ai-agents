# KSFE — Call Script & Objection Handling

Phone is the higher-yield channel for a PSU. Use the landline numbers in
`leads/ksfe-trivandrum-branches.csv`.

**Timing:** 10:30–13:00 or 14:30–16:00, working days. Avoid month-end and the
first week of the month — chitty auction and collection cycles make branches busy.

**Language:** open in Malayalam if you can. It matters here.

---

## Branch call — purpose is routing, not selling

> "Namaskaram. I'm [Name] from [Agency], a digital-marketing agency here in
> Thiruvananthapuram.
>
> I'm not selling anything on this call — I know branch offices don't handle
> marketing spend. I'm trying to reach whoever handles publicity or digital
> marketing for the Thiruvananthapuram region.
>
> We've put together a short audit of how KSFE chitties show up in online search
> compared to the private chit funds and gold-loan companies advertising in the
> city. I'd like to get it to the right person.
>
> Would that be the Regional Office, or does it go to Head Office at Thrissur?
> And do you have a name or a direct number I could use?"

**Then:**
- Note the name, designation and number in the CSV `notes` column.
- Ask: *"May I mention that you suggested I get in touch?"* — a referral from a
  branch materially raises reply rates at RO.
- If they engage, offer the free one-page local audit. That is the only thing
  you are trying to place at branch level.

## Regional Office call

> "Namaskaram. I'm [Name] from [Agency], a performance-marketing agency in
> Thiruvananthapuram. May I speak with whoever handles marketing or publicity
> for the region?
>
> [Once connected] — Thank you for taking the call. Briefly: we analysed digital
> advertising in Kerala's financial-services category this quarter. Private chit
> funds and gold-loan NBFCs are running continuous paid campaigns against the
> same Thiruvananthapuram audiences your branches serve. KSFE has the one
> advantage none of them can claim — the State Government guarantee — and it
> isn't showing up in those placements.
>
> We've written this into a short audit covering your Urban branches, including
> a costing for a Pravasi Chitty campaign targeted at Malayali audiences in the
> UAE and Saudi.
>
> I'd like to send it across, and if it's useful, present it in 30 minutes at
> your office. Could I have the right email address?
>
> One more thing — if regional media is procured through tender or GeM, could
> you point me to that process? We'd rather follow the correct route."

## Objections

**"Marketing is decided at Head Office / Thrissur."**
> "Understood, that's what I expected. Could I still send you the regional audit?
> If the Regional Office finds it useful, a note from you to Head Office carries
> far more weight than a cold approach from us. And could you share the right
> contact at Thrissur?"

**"We're a government company, we don't hire private agencies like this."**
> "Absolutely — and I'm not asking for a direct appointment. If this goes through
> tender or GeM, tell me where it's published and we'll bid formally. In the
> meantime the audit is free and yours to keep, whoever ends up executing it."

**"We already have an agency / we already advertise."**
> "Good to know. Most of what we see is print, hoardings and television — strong
> for awareness. What we measure is different: cost per verified enquiry, and
> attribution from an ad click through to an actual branch walk-in. If your
> current reporting already shows cost-per-enquiry by branch, you're ahead of
> most and I'll stop taking your time. If it doesn't, that's exactly the gap
> we'd fill — alongside your existing agency, not replacing them."

**"KSFE is well known, we don't need advertising."**
> "For brand awareness, completely agree — KSFE doesn't need an introduction in
> Kerala. The gap is at the moment of intent. When someone in Kazhakuttom
> searches 'chitty near me' or 'monthly savings scheme', the ads on that page
> are private operators. That person already trusts KSFE — they're just not
> being shown you at the point where they act."

**"Send an email, we'll get back."**
> "Of course. May I confirm the correct address, and your name for the subject
> line? I'll send it today, and I'll follow up next week if I haven't heard —
> is that alright?"
>
> *(Then actually follow up. Log the date in the CSV.)*

**"What does it cost?"**
> "The audit is free. For the pilot we'd propose a 90-day engagement on one
> region — a fixed monthly retainer plus media spend, with cost-per-enquiry as
> the reported KPI. I'd rather show you the numbers first and quote against a
> defined scope than give you a figure now that means nothing."

## Call log fields

Record against `branch_code` in the CSV:

`date · contact name · designation · direct number · outcome · next action · follow-up date`

## Do not

- Do not imply or promise returns on a chitty. Regulated product, regulated claims.
- Do not offer anything of value to an individual officer — this is a government
  body and that is a bribery exposure, not a sales tactic.
- Do not claim an existing relationship with KSFE you do not have.
- Do not call the toll-free customer helpline (1800 425 3455) for sales. It is a
  subscriber support line; using it for pitching gets you flagged.
