## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (confirmed by the owner). No framework, no build step for the live page. Lives in `coconeo-landing/`. Prices, packs and reviews are data files in `assets/js/` so the page can later be pasted into any store theme.

## Users

- **Kerala households** buying their regular cooking oil, used to oil from the local mill, judging by smell, colour and price per litre.
- **Malayalis outside Kerala** (Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Gulf) who miss home-style coconut oil and buy online.
- **Health-conscious urban buyers** comparing cold-pressed / virgin coconut oils from premium national brands (e.g. Tata Simply Better), reading labels and certifications before buying.

The job on the product page: decide this oil is pure and trustworthy enough to switch to, pick a pack size, and order (cart, Buy now, or WhatsApp).

## Product Purpose

Sell Coco Neo cold pressed coconut oil (1 L bottle, plus 2 L and 3 L value packs) direct to consumers from coconeo.in. Success is a completed order, and repeat orders of the bigger packs.

## Positioning

A small Kerala producer that presses the oil in its own ISO 22000:2018 certified, FSSAI-licensed unit in Neyyattinkara, Thiruvananthapuram. Cold pressed, single ingredient, no preservatives. The national brands cannot claim "made by us, in our own unit, in Kerala".

## Operating Context

- Coconut oil in Kerala is used daily for cooking (tempering, frying banana chips, curries), for the weekly hair-oiling ritual, and on skin.
- Buyers check that pure coconut oil solidifies below about 24°C as a purity test.
- Malayalam name on the label: വെളിച്ചെണ്ണ (velichenna).
- Ordering by phone/WhatsApp is normal for this audience; cash on delivery is expected.

Craft level of Tata Simply Better is the bar.

## Product Principles

1. Purity is shown, not asserted: point to the label, the certifications and the 24°C solidifying test.
2. Kerala first: home-style taste and smell is the reason to buy; speak to the kitchen, not to wellness jargon.
3. Never invent proof: no fabricated reviews, counts, awards or health claims.
4. Make ordering easy for every habit: cart, Buy now, WhatsApp, COD.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many buyers are older home cooks on mid-range Android phones: large tap targets, readable type sizes, fast-loading images, and plain language. Malayalam appears as the product name; the page copy is English.
