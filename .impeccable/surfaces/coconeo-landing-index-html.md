# Coco Neo product page

Scope: coconeo-landing/index.html. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: Kerala households, Malayalis outside Kerala and health-conscious urban buyers deciding to switch to this oil and ordering a pack.
Proof on hand: the label (ingredients, nutrition, ISO 22000:2018, FSSAI), the 24°C purity test, product photos. Reviews are placeholders until real ones arrive.
Unresolved: real prices, shelf life (5 vs 9 months), checkout URL, courier API.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The label becomes the page. The bottle stays on stage while the reader goes through what is printed on it; refuses the category's stock hero, icon-card benefits row and accordion FAQ.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, one stone tone step, espresso ink, seal-green action, oil-gold and palm-green full-width bands. Rokkitt 800 slab display, Literata text. Pills for controls, square corners for everything else, no lines outside real tables.
STORY: The visitor sees exactly what the oil is and what it costs, reads the label and process as printed tables, proves purity with the 24°C test, sees how Kerala uses it, reads reviews, and buys from the first screen, the price list, the uses band or the phone bar.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: fixed bottle photo in the left 47% with seven thumbnails below; right column holds the two-line capital title, one supporting sentence, price, rating, three pack squares, quantity and Add to cart. Phone: photo first, title and price in the first screen.
FORM: Owner took the standing exit (clean category standard, played straight; reference: Tata Simply Better), built in tasteskill's split-stage direction. Signature interaction: the 24°C slider. Motion grammar: section entrances, stage crossfade and drift, footer name settling. seed: 516959f1fe3a96c0
