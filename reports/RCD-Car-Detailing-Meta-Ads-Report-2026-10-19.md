## 4. Analysis
- **Underbody is the workhorse.** It took 82% of spend and delivered 81% of conversations at ₹19.61, close to the campaign average. At ₹56 CPM it is also the cheapest reach.
- **Graphene is the most efficient but barely tested.** It has the best CTR (4.59%) and the lowest cost per conversation (₹12.87), but only 13 conversations on ₹167. It also has the highest CPM (₹101). Treat it as a promising signal, not proof.
- **The interior + wash ad (1500 pack) is the weakest.** Its cost per conversation is 32% above the campaign average and its CTR is the lowest of the three ads with meaningful delivery. It is a low-ticket offer (₹1,588) with a small-and-medium-cars-only restriction, so the audience may be narrower.
- **The testimonial ad has not been given a fair test.** Delivery was only 213 impressions, so no conclusion can be drawn. Meta's allocation is favouring the other ads.
- **Frequency is healthy at 1.98.** The anomaly check found no creative fatigue, narrow-audience or auction-overlap issues.
- **Tracking gap.** The only outcome measured is WhatsApp conversations started. No bookings, revenue or ROAS data is available from Meta, so true return cannot be calculated yet.

## 5. Recommendations
1. **Protect Underbody** as the core ad, and refresh the creative within about 2 weeks, once frequency passes about 2.5.
2. **Scale Graphene gradually.** Increase its share of budget by about 20% every 2 to 3 days. If cost per conversation stays under about ₹18 after 40 or more conversations, make it a second core ad. It has a high ticket price (₹4,989), so the leads are potentially the most valuable.
3. **Fix or replace the interior + wash ad.** Try a new hook or visual, or test it in its own ad set with a narrower local radius around Ulloor, Trivandrum. Pause it if cost per conversation stays above ₹25 after another ~₹500 of spend.
4. **Give the testimonial ad a fair test.** Move it to a separate ad set with a small dedicated budget of about ₹100 to ₹150 per day for 3 days.
5. **Close the loop on revenue.** Have the RCD team log each WhatsApp lead as booked or not booked, by source ad. Then judge ads on cost per booking rather than cost per conversation.
6. **Use the budget.** The campaign is spending below its ₹450 daily cap. Once the new creatives are in, check delivery and consider raising the cap if cost per conversation holds.
7. **Add creative variety.** Candidates are before-and-after reels, a ceramic coating offer, and a seasonal monsoon underbody message.
