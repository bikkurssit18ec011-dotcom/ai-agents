    Questions

    Why has my oil turned white and solid? Pure coconut oil sets below about 24°C. Stand the bottle in lukewarm water for a few minutes and it turns clear again. Quality is not affected.

    Can I deep fry with it? Yes. Kerala has always fried banana chips and pappadam in coconut oil. Avoid reusing the same oil many times.

    Is it the same oil for hair and skin? Yes, one bottle does both. Patch-test on skin first if yours is sensitive.

    Is anything added to it? No. The only ingredient is coconut oil. No preservatives, colour or flavour.

    How should I store it? In a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight, with the cap closed. Use it before the date printed on the bottle.
