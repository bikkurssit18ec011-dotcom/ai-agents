Why does my coconut oil turn white and solid?

Pure coconut oil solidifies below about 24°C. This is natural and does not affect quality. Keep the bottle in lukewarm water for a few minutes to make it liquid again.

Can I use it for deep frying?

Yes. Coconut oil is traditionally used for frying in Kerala, including banana chips and pappadam. Avoid reusing the oil many times.

Is it suitable for hair and skin?

Yes — it is the same pure oil. Many customers use one bottle for cooking and their weekly hair oiling. Patch-test on skin first if you are sensitive.

Does it contain preservatives or added flavour?

No. The only ingredient is coconut oil.
